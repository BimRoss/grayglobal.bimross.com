The principal

David Glossop

A strategic development and sustainability executive with close to twenty years' international experience across major development, resources, transport, energy and infrastructure.

David has worked on the owner's side of programmes of real scale, where the questions are commercial, technical, regulatory and political at the same time, and where the cost of a wrong call is measured in years and billions. His background runs the full width of the sector: open-cut and underground mining, oil and gas, ports, civil and building works, and renewable energy. That breadth is the point. Complex decisions rarely sit inside one discipline, and the judgement that serves a client is the judgement that has seen how they connect.

When you engage Gray Global, you work with the principal who has sat in those decisions, not a team assembled after the contract is signed.

How we work

Four principles that shape every engagement.

01

Independent

We act for the party carrying the risk. We hold no position in the transaction and no scope to protect, so the advice is the advice.

02

Evidence-led

We separate what is tested from what is asserted, and we tell you which is which. No forecast we cannot stand behind in the room.

03

Multidisciplinary

Commercial, technical, regulatory and delivery perspectives in one judgement, because complex decisions do not respect the boundaries between them.

04

Senior

You deal with the principal throughout. The experience you are buying is the experience doing the work.
