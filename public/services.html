Services

Five ways we help you commit capital with your eyes open.

Each engagement is scoped to a decision. We give you an independent read on whether it holds, what it will take, and where it can go wrong, in language a board can act on.

01

Investment & Development Due Diligence

Before capital is committed, you need an honest answer to one question: is this as good as it looks, and what have we not priced? We provide independent assessment of the opportunity, the constraints that will shape it, the key risks, the real value drivers, and the development pathways actually open to you.

We act for the party carrying the risk, whether that is the equity, the lender, or the owner. The output is evidence-led and plain: what we would rely on, what we would test further, and what would change the decision.

Opportunity assessmentTechnical due diligenceRisk & value driversLender / IC support
02

Development Strategy & Feasibility

A good development strategy survives contact with reality. We work through feasibility, business cases, master planning, commercial structuring and the development pathway, stress-testing the assumptions that the whole case rests on rather than the ones that are comfortable to revisit.

The result is a strategy you can defend to a board and a sequence you can actually fund and deliver, with the trade-offs made explicit rather than buried.

FeasibilityBusiness caseMaster planningCommercial structuring
03

Infrastructure & Enabling Works

Most development risk hides in the enabling works: the power, water, access, rail, port and services a project assumes will be there. We map infrastructure requirements, the interfaces and dependencies between them, and the delivery sequencing that keeps a programme from stalling on a connection nobody owned.

This is where an integrated mine, rail and port, or a precinct and its services, either hangs together or comes apart. We make sure it hangs together on paper before it has to on site.

Enabling worksInterfaces & dependenciesDelivery sequencingIntegrated systems
04

Planning, Environment, Sustainability & Approvals

Approvals are not a formality to be managed at the end. They set the timeline, the cost of carry, and sometimes whether the project happens at all. We develop environmental and sustainability strategy, read planning and permitting risk early, and build a clear path through the regulatory and stakeholder landscape.

The aim is a credible route to approval and a social licence that holds, with the risks that could derail either one surfaced while there is still time to act on them.

Environmental strategyPermitting & approvalsPlanning riskStakeholder & social licence
05

Executive & Owner-Side Advisory

When the advice around the table all comes from parties with something to sell, an independent voice is worth having. We provide executive and owner-side advisory: independent challenge, oversight of consultants and contractors, and clear recommendations for executives, boards and investment committees.

We are the owner's eyes on the programme, and the independent read when the internal view and the contractor's view no longer agree.

Owner-side advisoryIndependent challengeConsultant oversightBoard & IC recommendations
